# Prototype Brief

## Purpose
Prove that a personal music graph can generate genuinely useful, non-obvious insight for one music lover before building social, marketplace or multi-user features.

## Product thesis
A social music journal and personal music graph for people who care deeply about music. The product should help people understand, explore and deepen their relationship with music by combining intentional taste, listening behaviour, physical ownership and music knowledge.

Internal shorthand: Letterboxd-style identity and community + Discogs-level collecting knowledge + an intelligent personal music graph.

## Prototype must demonstrate
1. My Music — a profile/journal of owned, heard, loved and explored music.
2. My Graph — navigable relationships among artists, releases, labels, genres, scenes and collaborators.
3. Complete / Gaps — meaningful omissions in catalogues and areas the user cares about.
4. Dig for Me — non-obvious discoveries with an explanation of why they matter.
5. Record Shop Mode — a fast answer to: do I already own this, and is this a different pressing?

## Validation criterion
After seeding a slice of a believable musical life, the prototype should surface several observations or recommendations that feel true, useful and non-obvious, and that lead somewhere the user genuinely wants to explore next.

## Deliberately out of scope for now
- Marketplace and payments
- Dealer features
- Full social network mechanics
- Production-scale recommendation infrastructure
- Comprehensive proprietary music database
- Five production-grade integrations before validation
