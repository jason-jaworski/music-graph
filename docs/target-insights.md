# Target Insights

The prototype should be designed around these behaviours, not generic recommendation features.

1. **Duplicate prevention** — You already own this release, or you own a different pressing.
2. **Artist catalogue gaps** — You love this artist but are missing these important releases.
3. **Label gaps** — You own several releases from this label but have not explored adjacent ones.
4. **Scene gaps** — You appear to like this scene or era but have barely explored connected artists.
5. **Producer / collaborator threads** — Several records you love share a producer, engineer or musician.
6. **Underexplored artist** — You repeatedly listen to this artist but have only heard a fraction of their catalogue.
7. **Owned but neglected** — You own this record but rarely return to it.
8. **Deep-cut discovery** — This obscure release is unusually connected to music you already love.
9. **Collector completeness** — You have most of the core releases in a catalogue, series or label run.
10. **Taste blind spot** — Your collection clusters strongly around two areas with a conspicuous missing branch between them.

## First five prototype behaviours
- Do I own this?
- What am I missing from this artist?
- What thread should I follow next?
- What obscure record should I investigate?
- What does my collection say about my taste that I have not noticed?

## Design requirement
Every recommendation or observation should explain its reasoning. The product should help the user understand *why* something matters, not merely rank items.
