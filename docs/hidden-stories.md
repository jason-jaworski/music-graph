# Hidden Stories in the Validation Dataset

The synthetic collector is not random. It contains deliberate patterns that the prototype should be able to discover without those answers being hard-coded into the UI.

## 1. The Jesus Lizard prehistory
Strong love/ownership signals for The Jesus Lizard should make Scratch Acid unusually relevant. The graph should eventually explain the path through shared members David Yow and David Wm. Sims.

Expected behaviour: **Dig for Me / follow a thread**.

## 2. The Dischord gap
The collector strongly likes Fugazi and has Minor Threat, but Rites of Spring and Embrace are absent. This should surface as a meaningful catalogue/scene gap rather than simply recommending more post-hardcore.

Expected behaviour: **What am I missing? / taste blind spot**.

## 3. The Albini thread
Big Black and Shellac are strong explicit taste signals. The collection also contains several records that can connect through Steve Albini's recording/production work once credits are verified and added to the factual graph.

Expected behaviour: **What does my collection say about my taste that I haven't noticed?**

Important: do not activate this story until individual credit edges have been verified.

## 4. The Talk Talk branch
The collector strongly loves the late Talk Talk run and also likes Bark Psychosis. Mark Hollis's solo album is deliberately unheard.

Expected behaviour: **Follow this thread**.

## 5. UK post-punk blind spot
The collection is dense around Wire, The Fall, Gang of Four, This Heat, PiL, Young Marble Giants and adjacent music, while The Pop Group and The Raincoats are deliberately absent.

Expected behaviour: **Taste blind spot / scene gap**.

## 6. Drone and heavy experimental branch
Strong signals around Sunn O))), Earth, Boris, Melvins, Sleep, Swans and Godflesh create a coherent area. Khanate is deliberately absent.

Expected behaviour: **Deep-cut discovery**.

## 7. Guitar minimalism branch
Sonic Youth and Glenn Branca are present. Rhys Chatham is absent.

Expected behaviour: **Follow a thread / deep-cut discovery**.

## Weak signals and red herrings
Mission of Burma, Faust and Neurosis are deliberately represented with weaker explicit signals. The ranking system should not treat every plausible graph edge as equally important.

# Validation principle
The prototype passes when it can reconstruct several of these stories from graph evidence and explain them persuasively. It fails if the result is simply a list of adjacent artists.
